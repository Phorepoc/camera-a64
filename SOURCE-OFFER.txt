Camera drivers for the PinePhone (camera-a64) -- licence notice and source offer

This package contains camera drivers from the Linux kernel (sun6i-csi, ov5640, gc2145, i2c-gpio, i2c-algo-bit),
licensed under the GNU General Public License version 2 (GPL-2.0), built together with our compatibility layer and
glue, which are GPL-2.0-only as well. The licence texts are installed with this package.

Corresponding source, available to anyone who has this package, for at least three years after the release you
installed it from:

  1. The unmodified Linux files, from https://codeberg.org/megi/linux at commit
     88af81e226a88ae422a9c301246e7ae64cda0b75, each pinned by SHA-256 in linux/SHA256SUMS of the repository below.
  2. Our files and the scripts that build and package the drivers:
       https://github.com/Phorepoc/camera-a64   (the release tag of this package)
     with the toolchain images and build tools in https://github.com/Phorepoc/toolchain and https://github.com/Phorepoc/pdk.

Written offer: if the links above are no longer available, send a request to the address given on the Phorepoc
organisation page https://github.com/Phorepoc and we will send you the corresponding source on a medium of your choice
for the cost of the medium.
